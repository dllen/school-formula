US Customary Unit Conversions

Printable US customary unit conversion table covering length (inches to miles), volume (fluid ounces to gallons) and mass (ounces to short tons). Free.

This chart converts between US customary units and metric. Each row is a single equality - 1 inch is 2.54 cm, 1 gallon is about 3.785 L, 1 pound is about 453.6 g - so a problem that crosses the two systems becomes a single read instead of a chain of conversions. The chart is intended to sit on a desk next to a homework book so the values do not need to be memorised; they are looked up the moment they are needed.

How to use it

  • Read across the row to find the metric equivalent of a customary value, or multiply by the equality to convert in the other direction.
  • Round to the precision the problem needs: a cooking conversion in cups is fine to the nearest tenth of a litre, but a science experiment needs the precise 29.574 ml per fluid ounce.
  • For mixed-unit problems, chain conversions: 5 ft 4 in is 5 × 30.48 + 4 × 2.54 cm.
  • Use the chart as a sanity check during science experiments where you need an approximate metric value quickly.
  • Print the chart on a single sheet so a child can refer to it while measuring with a customary ruler or scale.
  • Use the chart for cooking conversions: 1 cup to millilitres, 1 ounce to grams for butter or flour.

Frequently asked questions

What is the US customary system?

It is the system used in the United States for everyday measurement: inches, pounds, gallons.

Why does the chart convert to metric, not to other customary units?

Because the metric system is the international default, and every problem that crosses a border, a textbook, or a science class needs the metric value.

Why are some values exact and some approximate?

The inch and the pound are defined by the metric system exactly; volumes and mass conversions to the short ton are approximate.

What is a short ton?

It is the US customary ton of 2,000 pounds, about 907 kg. The metric tonne (1,000 kg) is heavier.

Does this chart include temperature conversions?

No. Temperature converts with a formula, not a single equality per degree.

Why is 1 ounce defined as 28.350 g rather than a nicer number?

Because the avoirdupois ounce is defined as exactly 28.349523125 grams, and 28.350 is the value rounded to three decimal places.

Is the UK gallon the same as the US gallon?

No. The UK (imperial) gallon is about 4.546 L; the US gallon is about 3.785 L. The chart lists the US gallon because most US problems use it.

How do I convert a weight in pounds to kilograms without the chart?

Multiply by 0.45359237. The chart rounds this to the nearest tenth of a kilogram, which is close enough for most everyday purposes.
