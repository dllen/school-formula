现在进行时

英语

be+动词ing形式，表达正在发生的动作。

📚 系统教程

🎯 本课目标

  • Can make present continuous sentences with "be + verb-ing" for actions happening now
  • Can spell the -ing form correctly (playing, making, running, swimming)
  • Can tell the difference between present simple (habits) and present continuous (happening now)
  • Can use time clues like "now, right now, look!, listen!" to choose the present continuous

📖 知识讲解

The present continuous is your "live broadcast" tense — you use it for things that are happening right now, at this very moment. If you can see it going on, use this tense.

Structure: am / is / are + verb-ing

  • "I am reading a book (now)."
  • "She is singing a song (look!)."
  • "They are playing football (right now)."

How to make the -ing form:

  • Most verbs: just add -ing → play → playing, read → reading
  • Silent e at the end: drop the e, add -ing → make → making, dance → dancing
  • Short vowel + one consonant: double the consonant → run → running, swim → swiming, sit → sitting

Present simple vs. present continuous:

  • Present simple = habits: "I play football every Saturday."
  • Present continuous = happening now: "I am playing football (now)."
  • Clue words for present continuous: now, right now, at the moment, look!, listen!

Real-life tip: when you watch a cartoon in English, pause it and say what is happening: "The cat is running. The dog is sleeping."

✏️ 例题精讲

基础：变成 -ing 形式

题目：Write the -ing form: make, run, write, swim.

解答：making, running, writing, swimming.

💡 make → drop e; run/swim → double the last letter; write → drop e.

进阶：用现在进行时造句

题目：Look at the action: "The boy / football / now." Make a sentence.

解答：The boy is playing football now.

💡 The boy (he) → is; play → playing; add "now".

挑战：区分两种时态

题目：Which tense? "She ____ (dance) in the room now." vs "She ____ (dance) every day."

解答：First: "is dancing" (now → present continuous). Second: "dances" (every day → present simple, third person).

💡 "now" → continuous (be + -ing); "every day" → simple (verb + s).

🧩 亲子互动

🎬 "动作直播员" Live Action Reporter（约 8 分钟）

  1. 家长做一个动作（如喝水、看书），孩子当"直播员"用英语解说："You are drinking water!"
  2. 孩子做动作，家长来解说，然后一起改成否定句（"I'm not sleeping"）和疑问句（"Are you reading?"）。
  3. 进阶：家长说一个习惯（"I play basketball every Sunday"），孩子立刻说一个"现在"的动作（"I am talking now"）作对比。
  4. 挑战：看一段无声动画片，孩子用英语"直播"画面里正在发生的事。

💡 目的：把"be + v-ing"和"此刻正在发生"建立直接联系，用身体动作强化记忆。

📝 课后练习

第 1 题：写出下列动词的 -ing 形式：take, sit, look, run。

✅ 答案：taking, sitting, looking, running

解析：take 去 e；sit/run 双写末字母；look 直接加 ing。

第 2 题：用所给词的正确形式填空：Listen! She ____ (sing).

✅ 答案：is singing

解析：Listen! 提示正在发生，用现在进行时。

第 3 题：改错：They is playing games now.

✅ 答案：They are playing games now.

解析：They 是复数，be 动词用 are。

第 4 题：用现在进行时描述：你的爸爸妈妈现在在做什么？（造一个句子）

✅ 答案：开放性题，如 "My father is reading a newspaper."

解析：am/is/are + v-ing 表示此刻正在进行的动作。
