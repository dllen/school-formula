数字单词

英语

one到twenty，认读与拼写数字单词。

📚 系统教程

🎯 本课目标

  • Can read, spell and say the number words from one to twenty
  • Can understand spoken numbers and point to the correct numeral
  • Can use number words in everyday English (age, counting objects)
  • Can ask and answer "How many …?" using number words

📖 知识讲解

Numbers are one of the most practical vocabulary groups in English. You need them for your age, the time, counting toys, and shopping. Let us learn them step by step.

1 to 12 — each is a unique word to memorise: one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, eleven, twelve

13 to 19 — most end in -teen (think: "teenager"!): thirteen, fourteen, fifteen, sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen Watch out: thirteen (not "threeteen"), fifteen (not "fiveteen").

The tens — end in -ty: twenty (20). Later you will meet thirty, forty, fifty …

Patterns that help:

  • 13–19: add -teen to the base number (with small spelling changes).
  • 20, 30, 40 …: add -ty (twen-ty, thir-ty, for-ty).

Using numbers in English:

  • "How many apples?" → "Five apples."
  • "I am nine years old."
  • "My phone number is …" (read each digit separately: "three one zero …")

Real-life idea: count your steps in English when you walk downstairs — "one, two, three …" — a great daily drill!

✏️ 例题精讲

基础：数字与单词配对

题目：Write the English word for 15 and 20.

解答：15 = fifteen; 20 = twenty.

💡 15 ends in -teen (fifteen); 20 ends in -ty (twenty).

进阶：用 How many 问答

题目：A: "How many pencils are on the desk?" (There are 8.) B: "______"

解答："There are eight pencils." or simply "Eight."

💡 "How many + 复数名词?" asks about quantity — answer with a number.

挑战：听数字、写单词

题目：Your teacher says "seventeen". Write the numeral and the word.

解答：Numeral: 17. Word: seventeen.

💡 The -teen ending tells you it is in the teens (13–19).

🧩 亲子互动

🔢 "数字跳数" Number Jump（约 6 分钟）

  1. 在地上贴 10 张数字卡（1–10），家长说一个英文数字，孩子跳到对应的卡片上。
  2. 进阶：家长说 "three plus four"，孩子先算出 7 再跳到 7。
  3. 倒数挑战：从 ten 倒数到 one，每数一个做一个蹲起。
  4. 生活应用：让孩子用英语报出自己的年龄、门牌号（逐位读）。

💡 目的：把数字单词和"听-说-动作"结合，建立快速的英语数感反应。

📝 课后练习

第 1 题：写出 11、14、18 的英文单词。

✅ 答案：eleven, fourteen, eighteen

解析：11 = eleven（特殊）；14 = fourteen；18 = eighteen（注意只有一个 t）。

第 2 题：How many books are on your desk? (根据实际情况回答)

✅ 答案：开放性题，如 "There are six books."

解析：用 "There are + 数字 + 复数名词" 回答数量。

第 3 题：twenty 是数字几？

✅ 答案：20

解析：twenty = 20。

第 4 题：判断：thirteen 和 thirty 是同一个数吗？

✅ 答案：不是。thirteen = 13，thirty = 30。

解析：-teen 是十几，-ty 是几十，注意区分。
