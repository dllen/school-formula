📚 系统教程

🎯 本课目标

  • Can understand that phonetic symbols are a code for sounds, not new letters
  • Can read common vowel and consonant symbols such as /æ/, /iː/, /ʃ/, /θ/
  • Can look up the pronunciation of a new word in a dictionary using its phonetic symbols
  • Can use phonics and phonetic symbols together to read unfamiliar words

📖 知识讲解

Phonetic symbols (音标) are like a secret code for sounds. The same English letter can make different sounds in different words (think of "a" in "cat", "cake" and "car"). Phonetic symbols solve this problem — one symbol always means one sound, so you can read any word correctly, even the first time you see it.

There are 48 symbols in the International Phonetic Alphabet for English: 20 vowel sounds and 28 consonant sounds. In primary school, you do not need to master all 48 at once — start with the ones that confuse you most.

A few symbols to begin with:

  • /æ/ — the short a in cat
  • /iː/ — the long ee in sheep
  • /ʃ/ — the "sh" sound in ship
  • /θ/ — the "th" sound in think (stick your tongue out!)
  • /ð/ — the "th" sound in this
  • /ɒ/ — the short o in dog

How to use them: when you look up a word in a dictionary, you will see symbols in slashes / / or brackets [ ]. Read the symbols one by one, then blend the sounds. This works even for brand-new words — that is the power of phonetic symbols!

✏️ 例题精讲

基础：说出音标对应的单词

题目：You see the symbol /iː/. Which English sound does it match? Give a word that uses it.

解答：/iː/ is the long "ee" sound, like in "sheep", "feet", "tree".

💡 The colon (ː) means the sound is long — hold it a little longer.

进阶：借助音标读生词

题目：A dictionary shows "mouth" → /maʊθ/. Read it using the symbols.

解答：/m/ + /aʊ/ + /θ/ → "mouth". The /θ/ needs your tongue between your teeth.

💡 Read each symbol in order, then blend — just like phonics, but with symbols instead of letters.

挑战：区分两个容易混淆的音

题目：Which word has /θ/ and which has /ð/: "think" or "this"?

解答："think" has /θ/ (unvoiced — no vibration); "this" has /ð/ (voiced — your throat vibrates).

💡 Put your fingers gently on your throat: if you feel a buzz, it is the voiced /ð/; if not, it is /θ/.

🧩 亲子互动

🎙️ "音标镜子练习" Mirror Sound Lab（约 8 分钟）

  1. 家长和孩子各拿一面小镜子，看自己的嘴巴。家长说一个音标（如 /θ/），孩子观察并模仿：舌尖要伸出一点放在上下齿之间。
  2. 家长举起单词卡（ship, sheep, think, this, dog, door），孩子先"破译"音标（如果卡片背面有音标）再读出单词。
  3. 对比游戏：家长读两对单词 "sip/zip" 和 "thin/tin"，孩子听辨首音不同，并用镜子检查自己的舌头和嘴唇位置。
  4. 挑战：孩子选一个自己容易读错的单词，查字典抄下音标，教家长读。

💡 目的：把"看不见的声音"变成"看得见的口型"，用镜子建立正确的发音肌肉记忆。

📝 课后练习

第 1 题：英语国际音标一共有多少个？

✅ 答案：48 个（20 个元音，28 个辅音）。

解析：这是国际音标（IPA）中英语使用的标准数量。

第 2 题：音标 /ʃ/ 对应哪个字母组合？举一个例词。

✅ 答案：对应字母组合 sh，如 ship。

解析：/ʃ/ 就是 "shh" 的声音。

第 3 题：下面哪个单词含 /ð/？think, this, three

✅ 答案：this

解析：this 中的 th 发浊音 /ð/；think 和 three 发清音 /θ/。

第 4 题：查字典写出 "apple" 的音标。

✅ 答案：/ˈæpl/

解析：第一个元音是 /æ/，注意重音符号 ˈ 在第一个音节前。
