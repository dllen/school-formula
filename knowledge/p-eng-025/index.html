一般将来时

英语

will与be going to表示将要发生的动作。

📚 系统教程

🎯 本课目标

  • Can make future sentences with "will + verb" for quick decisions, promises and predictions
  • Can make future sentences with "be going to + verb" for plans and intentions
  • Can spot future time words: tomorrow, next week/month/year, tonight, soon
  • Can use the negative forms "will not (won't)" and "be not going to"

📖 知识讲解

The future simple lets you talk about things that have not happened yet — your plans, hopes and predictions. In English, you have two handy ways to build it.

Structure 1: will + verb

  • Use for: quick decisions, promises, offers, predictions.
  • "I will help you." (offer) / "It will rain tomorrow." (prediction)
  • Negative: "will not" → won't. "I won't be late."
  • Question: "Will you come?" → "Yes, I will. / No, I won't."

Structure 2: be going to + verb

  • Use for: plans and intentions you decided before.
  • "I am going to visit my grandma next Sunday." (a plan)
  • "Look at those clouds! It is going to rain." (evidence now)

Time clues for the future: tomorrow, tonight, next week / month / year, in the future, soon, in 2030

Real-life tip: at bedtime, say one thing you will do tomorrow in English — "I will read a story tomorrow." That is a real future sentence!

✏️ 例题精讲

基础：用 will 造句

题目：You promise your mum you will finish homework. Say it in English.

解答："I will finish my homework."

💡 "will + 动词原形" 表示承诺或将来要做的事，动词不加 s。

进阶：用 be going to 表达计划

题目：You plan to see a film this weekend. Say it with "be going to".

解答："I am going to see a film this weekend."

💡 be going to 表示事先计划好的事，注意 be 要和主语一致（I am / she is / they are）。

挑战：变成否定和疑问

题目：Change to negative and question: "She will come tomorrow."

解答：Negative: "She won't come tomorrow." Question: "Will she come tomorrow?"

💡 will 的否定用 won't；疑问句把 will 提到主语前。

🧩 亲子互动

🔮 "未来愿望瓶" Future Wish Jar（约 8 分钟）

  1. 家长和孩子各拿 3 张纸条，用将来时写下/画下"明天的计划"，如 "I am going to play football."
  2. 把纸条折好放进"愿望瓶"，轮流抽取并大声读出句子。
  3. 进阶：抽到后把它变成否定句和疑问句（"I won't play football." / "Will you play football?"）。
  4. 挑战：用 will 许一个"周末愿望"，家长和孩子互相用 "Will you…?" 提问。

💡 目的：通过"写计划—读计划—变句型"的闭环，把 will 和 be going to 的结构练熟。

📝 课后练习

第 1 题：用 will 翻译：我明天会早点起床。

✅ 答案：I will get up early tomorrow.

解析：will + 动词原形 get up 表示将来。

第 2 题：把下列句子改为否定句：He is going to swim.

✅ 答案：He isn't going to swim. / He's not going to swim.

解析：be going to 的否定在 be 后加 not。

第 3 题：写出 "will not" 的缩写形式。

✅ 答案：won't

解析：will not 缩写为 won't。

第 4 题：用 be going to 造句：我打算下周末去看望奶奶。

✅ 答案：I am going to visit my grandma next weekend.

解析：be going to + 动词原形 表示计划。
