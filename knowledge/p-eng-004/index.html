英语

基本语法

名词单复数、人称代词、be动词、一般现在时、现在进行时。

系统教程

本课目标

  • Can choose the correct form of "be" (am / is / are) to match the subject
  • Can change a singular noun to its plural form with -s / -es and recognise common irregular ones
  • Can make a present continuous sentence with "be + verb-ing" for an action happening now
  • Can use personal pronouns (I, you, he, she, it, we, they) to replace nouns

知识讲解

Grammar is like the "rules of the road" for sentences — once you know them, you can say things clearly and correctly. In this topic we learn four small but powerful rules.

1. The verb "be" (am / is / are) — it changes shape depending on who we are talking about:

  • I am (I'm) → I am a student.
  • You are (You're) → You are my friend. (one person OR many)
  • He / She / It is (He's) → He is tall. She is kind. It is a cat.
  • We / They are (We're) → We are happy. They are teachers. Remember the rhyme: "I 用 am，you 用 are，is 连着他、她、它。"

2. Personal pronouns — tiny words that replace names so we do not repeat them:

  • I, you, he, she, it, we, they (subject pronouns).
  • Example: "Lily is 8. She is a student." (instead of "Lily is a student" again.)

3. Singular and plural nouns — how to show "one" vs. "more than one":

  • Most nouns: add -s (book → books, pen → pens).
  • After s, x, sh, ch: add -es (box → boxes, watch → watches).
  • Irregular: you just have to remember — man → men, child → children, foot → feet.

4. Present continuous (现在进行时) — for an action happening RIGHT NOW:

  • Structure: am / is / are + verb-ing.
  • "I am reading." "She is singing." "They are playing."
  • To make -ing: usually just add -ing (play → playing); drop a silent e (make → making); double the last letter in some short words (run → running, swim → swimming).

例题精讲

基础：选对 be 动词

题目：Choose the correct word: He ____ a teacher. (am / is / are)

解答：is. "He" goes with "is". → He is a teacher.

Singular subjects (he/she/it) use "is"; plural (we/they) use "are"; "I" uses "am".

进阶：名词变复数

题目：Write the plural of: bus, tomato, child, box.

解答：buses, tomatoes, children, boxes.

bus/box add -es (ends in s or x); tomato adds -es (ends in o, a few words do this); child is irregular (children).

挑战：用现在进行时写句子

题目：Look at this scene: "The boy / football / now." Make a sentence in the present continuous.

解答：The boy is playing football now.

Structure: subject (The boy) + is + verb-ing (playing) + the rest (football now). The word "now" is a clue for present continuous.

亲子互动

🏃 "语法快问快答" Grammar Lightning Race（约 8 分钟）

  1. 家长快速说一个主语（如 "I", "the cats", "my mother"），孩子抢答正确的 be 动词（am/is/are），答对得 1 分。
  2. 第二轮：家长说单数名词（如 "bus", "mouse"），孩子说出复数形式。
  3. 第三轮"动作猜猜猜"：家长做一个动作（如读书、喝水），孩子用现在进行时造句："You are drinking water." 家长答 "Yes, I am!" 或 "No, I'm not — I'm…"
  4. 最后 2 分钟：孩子当小老师，考家长三个题。

💡 目的：通过快速反应和动作游戏，把语法规则变成"肌肉记忆"，避免死记硬背。

课后练习

第 1 题：I ____ 10 years old. (am / is / are)

答案：am

解析："I" 永远和 "am" 搭配。

第 2 题：写出下列名词的复数：sheep, knife, baby, watch。

答案：sheep, knives, babies, watches

解析：sheep 单复数同形；knife 变 f 为 v 加 es；baby 辅音+y 变 i 加 es；watch 加 es。

第 3 题：用现在进行时改写句子：She reads a book every day. → She ____ a book now.

答案：is reading

解析："now" 表示正在发生，用 be + v-ing：is reading。

第 4 题：改错：They is playing games.

答案：They are playing games.

解析："They" 是复数，be 动词用 are，不用 is。

第 5 题：用人称代词替换划线部分："Tom and I" → ______

答案：We

解析："Tom and I" 是第一人称复数，用 we。

趣味角

冷知识

英语语法其实比中文简单！中文没有时态变化，却有量词（一只鸡、一条鱼、一张纸），英语没有量词，却有时态变化。

生活中的数学

英语中最常见的动词是"be"，它有三种形式：am、are、is。记住口诀："I用am，you用are，is连着他她它"。比如：I am happy. You are smart. He is tall.

互动问答

为什么"I am a student"有"a"，而"我是学生"没有"一个"？

点击揭晓答案

英语可数名词前面必须加冠词a/an或the，这是英语的规则。中文不需要量词。
