日期与月份

英语

January到December，星期的表达方式。

📚 系统教程

🎯 本课目标

  • Can read, spell and say the twelve months from January to December
  • Can say the days of the week: Monday through Sunday
  • Can say dates correctly using ordinal numbers: "January the first" or "the first of January"
  • Can ask and answer "When is your birthday?" and "What's the date today?"

📖 知识讲解

Dates and months are everyday English — you need them for birthdays, holidays, school events and planning. Let us learn them in an orderly way.

The twelve months: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December Tip: learn them in chunks of three and say them like a rhythm.

The days of the week: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday Monday to Friday are "weekdays"; Saturday and Sunday are the "weekend".

Saying dates — use ordinal numbers (序数词):

  • 1st = first, 2nd = second, 3rd = third, 4th = fourth … 21st = twenty-first, 31st = thirty-first
  • Two common patterns:
    • "January the first" (British style, spoken)
    • "The first of January" (British style, spoken)
    • "January 1st" (American style, written)

Key questions:

  • "When is your birthday?" → "It is in June." / "It is on June 5th."
  • "What day is it today?" → "It is Monday."
  • "What's the date today?" → "It is September 13th."

Real-life tip: put an English calendar on your wall and say the date every morning: "Today is Monday, September 13th."

✏️ 例题精讲

基础：说出月份和星期

题目：Which month comes after August? Which day comes after Friday?

解答：After August comes September. After Friday comes Saturday.

💡 Months: … August, September, October … Week: … Friday, Saturday, Sunday.

进阶：用序数词说日期

题目：How do you say "6 月 3 日" in English?

解答："June the third" or "the third of June" or "June 3rd".

💡 3 日 = the third (3rd); remember 1st, 2nd, 3rd are special (first, second, third).

挑战：回答关于生日的问题

题目：A: "When is your birthday?" B: (Your birthday is March 15th.) "______"

解答："It is on March the fifteenth." or "It is on March 15th."

💡 A full date uses "on + the + ordinal + of + month".

🧩 亲子互动

📅 "日历问答" Calendar Quiz（约 8 分钟）

  1. 家长指着英语日历上的一个月份，孩子快速说出英文，并拼读（如 "S-e-p-t-e-m-b-e-r"）。
  2. 进阶：家长说一个节日（如"国庆节"），孩子说出它所在的月份："October."
  3. 生日问答：家长问 "When is your birthday?" 孩子回答 "It is on …" 并说出具体日期。
  4. 挑战：孩子当"小导游"，用英语介绍未来一周的家庭计划："On Monday we are going to the park."

💡 目的：把月份、星期、日期和真实日历活动绑定，让时间表达变得实用、可说。

📝 课后练习

第 1 题："December" 是第几个月？

✅ 答案：第 12 个月（十二月）。

解析：December 是一年中的最后一个月。

第 2 题：写出星期一到星期日的英文。

✅ 答案：Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday

解析：一周七天，注意拼写和大小写。

第 3 题：5 月 1 日用英语怎么说？

✅ 答案：May the first / the first of May / May 1st

解析：日期用序数词 first（1st）。

第 4 题：What day is it today? (根据实际情况回答)

✅ 答案：开放性题，如 "It is Saturday."

解析：用 It is + 星期几 回答。
