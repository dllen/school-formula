动物单词

英语

cat/dog/rabbit等常见动物词汇。

📚 系统教程

🎯 本课目标

  • Can read, spell and say common animal words: cat, dog, rabbit, bird, fish, elephant, monkey, panda, tiger, lion
  • Can describe animals using "It is …" and "It can …" (big, small, fast, can fly)
  • Can ask and answer "What is it? / What animal is it?"
  • Can talk about favourite animals with "I like …"

📖 知识讲解

Animals are a favourite topic for young learners — and knowing their English names lets you talk about pets, zoo trips and nature shows.

Common animal words:

  • Pets: cat, dog, rabbit, fish, bird
  • Farm animals: cow, pig, horse, sheep, duck, chicken
  • Wild / zoo animals: elephant, monkey, panda, tiger, lion, giraffe, snake

Describing animals:

  • "The elephant is big." / "The rabbit is small."
  • "The bird can fly." / "The fish can swim."
  • "The panda is black and white."

Talking about favourites:

  • "What is your favourite animal?" → "I like pandas." or "My favourite animal is the panda."

Real-life idea: next time you visit a zoo or watch an animal video, name three animals in English and say one thing about each. "Look — a tiger! It is big and strong!"

✏️ 例题精讲

基础：说出动物的英文

题目：What is "熊猫" in English? What about "老虎"?

解答："熊猫" = panda; "老虎" = tiger.

💡 Link each animal to a picture — visual memory is strongest for animal words.

进阶：描述动物

题目：Describe a bird in English using "It is …" and "It can …".

解答："The bird is small. It can fly."

💡 Use "It is + 形容词" for appearance and "It can + 动词" for ability.

挑战：用英语介绍最喜欢的动物

题目：Say your favourite animal and two reasons in English.

解答：Sample: "I like dolphins. They are cute and they can swim fast."

💡 Pattern: I like + animal. They are + adjective. They can + verb.

🧩 亲子互动

🐾 "动物模仿秀" Animal Charades（约 8 分钟）

  1. 准备动物卡片，孩子抽一张，用动作和声音模仿这种动物（不能说话），家长猜英文单词。
  2. 交换角色：家长模仿，孩子猜并大声说出英文 "It is a cat!"
  3. 进阶：猜中后，孩子必须用一句话描述该动物："The cat is small. It can run fast."
  4. 挑战：孩子当"小导游"，带家长逛"家庭动物园"，用英语介绍 3 种动物。

💡 目的：通过模仿和描述，把动物单词、形容词和 can 句型同时练到。

📝 课后练习

第 1 题："兔子"用英语怎么说？

✅ 答案：rabbit

解析：rabbit = 兔子。

第 2 题：用英语描述：The elephant is ______ and ______.

✅ 答案：开放性题，如 "big; strong"

解析：用形容词描述大象的外形特征。

第 3 题：回答：What is your favourite animal?

✅ 答案：开放性题，如 "I like pandas."

解析：I like + 动物 是表达喜好的标准句型。

第 4 题：判断："Sheep" 的复数形式是 "sheeps"，对吗？

✅ 答案：不对，sheep 单复数同形，复数仍是 sheep。

解析：sheep 是不规则名词，单复数同形。
