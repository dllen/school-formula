基础词汇

英语

颜色、数字、家庭成员、身体部位、食物、动物、学校用品。

📚 系统教程

🎯 本课目标

  • Can read, spell and say the English words for colours, numbers, family members, body parts and food
  • Can group English words into topics (e.g. all fruit words together) to remember them faster
  • Can point to a real object and say its English name
  • Can use a simple sentence like "This is a …" or "I like …" with the new words

📖 知识讲解

Words are like tools in a toolbox. The more words you know, the more you can say! In this topic we collect the most useful basic vocabulary and sort it into groups so they are easy to remember.

Colours: red, blue, green, yellow, orange, purple, black, white, pink, brown. Try this: look around the room and name three colours in English — "The sky is blue, the apple is red, the leaf is green!"

Numbers: one, two, three … ten, then eleven, twelve … twenty. Numbers are everywhere: your age, your house number, the time on the clock.

Family: father, mother, brother, sister, grandfather, grandmother (grandma/grandpa for short). Together, grandfather and grandmother are called grandparents; father and mother are your parents.

Body: head, eye, ear, nose, mouth, hand, arm, leg, foot (feet). Touch your nose and say "nose" — that makes the word stick.

Food & Animals: apple, banana, rice, fish, bread … and cat, dog, rabbit, bird, elephant … — these are words children love because they connect to real life.

The best trick? Stick a word card on the real object (a "door" label on the door) and say it every time you pass.

✏️ 例题精讲

基础：按主题归类单词

题目：Which word does NOT belong: apple, banana, carrot, orange?

解答：carrot. Apple, banana and orange are fruit; carrot is a vegetable.

💡 Sorting words into groups (fruit vs. vegetable) makes them much easier to remember.

进阶：用单词说句子

题目：Look at the colours of three things in your room and make a sentence for each using "The … is …"

解答：Sample: "The book is red." "The bag is blue." "The chair is black."

💡 Use "The + thing + is + colour" to describe what you see around you.

挑战：数一数、说一说

题目：How many people are in your family? Say their relationships in English (e.g. father, mother, …).

解答：Sample: "There are four people in my family: my father, my mother, my brother and me."

💡 Start with the people you see every day — family words are the most personal and memorable.

🧩 亲子互动

🖍️ "单词闪卡接力" Flashcard Relay（约 8 分钟）

  1. 准备 15-20 张单词卡（一面图、一面英文），覆盖颜色、动物、食物三个主题。
  2. 家长快速举起卡片，孩子抢答英文单词，答对得 1 分。
  3. 进阶轮：孩子拿到卡片后必须用它说一个完整句子，如 "I like bananas." 或 "The cat is white." 说对额外加 1 分。
  4. 最后把所有卡片按主题分成三堆，和孩子一起检查分对了没有。

💡 目的：通过"看图说词"和"用词造句"两个层次，把被动识别变成主动输出。

📝 课后练习

第 1 题：把下列单词按类别分成三组：red, apple, dog, green, banana, cat。

✅ 答案：Colours: red, green. Fruit: apple, banana. Animals: dog, cat.

解析：每个单词属于一个主题，按主题归类是记忆词汇的好方法。

第 2 题：填空：My father and my mother are my ______.

✅ 答案：parents

解析：father 和 mother 合称 parents。

第 3 题：用英语说出你身体的五个部位。

✅ 答案：开放性题，如 head, eye, ear, nose, hand。

解析：指着自己的身体部位说出对应英文，联系实物加深记忆。

第 4 题：翻译：我有一个红色的苹果和两根黄色的香蕉。

✅ 答案：I have a red apple and two yellow bananas.

解析：注意颜色放在名词前（red apple），以及 banana 的复数加 s。

🌟 趣味角

🧊 冷知识

英语中颜色词汇非常丰富：红色就有red、crimson（深红）、scarlet（鲜红）、ruby（红宝石色）等十几种说法！

📖 生活中的数学

在英语中，颜色还代表情绪。"blue"是蓝色也是"忧郁"，"green"是绿色也是"嫉妒"（green with envy）。所以当你感觉blue的时候，其实是心情"蓝"了！

❓ 互动问答

"black tea"为什么不是"黑茶"？

▶点击揭晓答案

是红茶！英国人看到茶叶颜色深就叫black tea，中国人看茶汤颜色叫red tea。
