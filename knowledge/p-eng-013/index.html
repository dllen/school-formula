自我介绍

英语

My name is.../I am...等基本介绍用语。

📚 系统教程

🎯 本课目标

  • Can say own name using "My name is …" or "I'm …"
  • Can say own age with "I am … years old"
  • Can introduce oneself in a short paragraph (name, age, class, hobby)
  • Can ask another person's name with "What's your name?"

📖 知识讲解

Introducing yourself is one of the first real things you can do in a new language. In English, there are a few friendly, standard ways to do it.

Saying your name:

  • "My name is Lily." — the full, polite form.
  • "I'm Lily." — shorter and very common.
  • Both are correct; "I'm …" sounds more casual and natural in everyday chat.

Saying your age:

  • "I am eight years old." or "I'm eight."
  • Tip: just say the number — English speakers often drop "years old" in casual talk.

Putting it together (a mini self-introduction):

"Hello! My name is Lily. I am eight years old. I study in Class 2, Grade 3. I like drawing and reading. Nice to meet you!"

Asking others:

  • "What's your name?" (What is your name?)
  • "How old are you?"

Real-life tip: practise a 30-second self-introduction and use it the first day of a new English class — it makes a great first impression!

✏️ 例题精讲

基础：介绍自己的名字

题目：How do you say "我叫王明" in English?

解答："My name is Wang Ming." or "I'm Wang Ming."

💡 Both are correct; "I'm Wang Ming" is more casual and natural.

进阶：介绍年龄

题目：How do you say "我今年 9 岁"?

解答："I am nine years old." or simply "I'm nine."

💡 "years old" can be left out in everyday conversation.

挑战：做一个完整的自我介绍

题目：Introduce yourself in English. Include: name, age and one hobby.

解答：Sample: "Hi! I'm Tom. I'm ten years old. I like playing football."

💡 Use the pattern: name → age → hobby. Keep it simple and smile!

🧩 亲子互动

🌟 "自我介绍接龙" Self-Intro Circle（约 8 分钟）

  1. 家长和孩子轮流用英语自我介绍，每人说 3 句（名字、年龄、爱好）。
  2. 第二轮升级：在第 2 句后加一个新信息，如班级 "I'm in Class 4, Grade 2." 或最喜欢的水果 "I like apples."
  3. 玩"猜一猜"：家长用英语介绍一位动画人物的名字和年龄（"My name is Peppa. I'm four."），孩子猜是谁。
  4. 挑战：孩子对着镜子做一个完整的 30 秒英语自我介绍，录下来回听。

💡 目的：把固定句型（My name is… / I'm…）通过反复输出变成自然表达，克服开口紧张。

📝 课后练习

第 1 题：你想告诉新同学"我叫小红"，用英语怎么说？

✅ 答案：My name is Xiaohong. / I'm Xiaohong.

解析：My name is 和 I'm 都可以介绍名字。

第 2 题：翻译：I am seven years old. → 中文是什么意思？

✅ 答案：我今年 7 岁。

解析：I am + 数字 + years old 表示年龄。

第 3 题：补全自我介绍：Hello! ____ Tom. ____ 9 years old.

✅ 答案：My name is / I'm; I'm

解析：第一空介绍名字，第二空介绍年龄。

第 4 题：你想知道新朋友的名字，应该问什么？

✅ 答案：What's your name?

解析：What's your name? 是询问对方名字的标准句型。
