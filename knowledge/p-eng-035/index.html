购物对话

英语

How much is it?等购物场景用语。

📚 系统教程

🎯 本课目标

  • Can ask about prices with "How much is / are …?"
  • Can use common shopping phrases: "I'd like … / Can I help you? / Here you are"
  • Can say numbers and prices in English (yuan / dollars)
  • Can role-play a complete shopping dialogue between shop assistant and customer

📖 知识讲解

Shopping dialogues are one of the most useful real-life English skills. Whether you are at a shop, a market or a restaurant, the same patterns come up again and again.

Key shopping phrases:

  • Shop assistant: "Can I help you?" / "What can I do for you?"
  • Customer: "I'd like a pencil, please." / "I want some apples."
  • Asking the price: "How much is the book?" / "How much are the apples?"
  • Saying the price: "It is 5 yuan." / "They are 10 yuan."
  • Paying: "Here you are." → "Thank you."

A complete shopping dialogue:

A (shop assistant): "Can I help you?" B (customer): "Yes, I'd like some bananas, please." A: "Here you are." B: "How much are they?" A: "They are six yuan." B: "Here you are. Thank you!"

Please and thank you make every exchange polite — always add "please" when you ask for something!

Real-life tip: practise at home by "buying" snacks from your parents in English before you eat them!

✏️ 例题精讲

基础：询问价格

题目：You want to know the price of an ice cream. What do you say?

解答："How much is the ice cream?"

💡 单数物品用 "How much is …?"；复数用 "How much are …?"

进阶：用 I'd like 表达想要

题目：Say in English: "我想要一瓶水。"

解答："I'd like a bottle of water, please."

💡 "I'd like = I would like"，是礼貌表达"我想要"的句型。

挑战：完成一段购物对话

题目：Complete: A: "Can I help you?" B: "______" (You want a red schoolbag.)

解答："I'd like a red schoolbag, please."

💡 礼貌回答：I'd like + 想要的东西 + please.

🧩 亲子互动

🛍️ "迷你小商店" Mini Shop Role-play（约 10 分钟）

  1. 把家里的文具、水果、零食摆成"小商店"，孩子当店员，家长当顾客（用英语）。
  2. 完整演练："Can I help you?" → "I'd like …" → "Here you are." → "How much is it?" → "It is … yuan."
  3. 进阶：角色互换，孩子当顾客，家长当店员；加入"太贵了" "It is too expensive!" 的还价表达。
  4. 挑战：用英语"买"3 件不同物品，计算总价并用英语报出。

💡 目的：在模拟购物情境中反复操练核心句型，让"Can I help you?"和"How much…?"变成自然反应。

📝 课后练习

第 1 题："How much are the apples?" 问的是什么？

✅ 答案：问苹果多少钱（价格）。

解析：How much 用来询问价格。

第 2 题：用英语说：我想买一件 T 恤。

✅ 答案：I'd like a T-shirt, please.

解析：I'd like + 名词 + please 是购物时的礼貌表达。

第 3 题：店员问你 "Can I help you?"，你想买一瓶牛奶，怎么回答？

✅ 答案：Yes, I'd like a bottle of milk, please.

解析：先肯定回答 Yes，再用 I'd like 说出想要的东西。

第 4 题：判断："How much is the shoes?" 对吗？

✅ 答案：不对，shoes 是复数，应该说 "How much are the shoes?"

解析：复数名词用 are，单数用 is。
