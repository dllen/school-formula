字母认读

英语

26个英文字母的大小写与发音。

📚 系统教程

🎯 本课目标

  • Can recognise and write all 26 letters in both upper case and lower case
  • Can say the alphabet in order from A to Z
  • Can tell which letters are vowels and which are consonants
  • Can find English letters and words in everyday life (on signs, packaging, books)

📖 知识讲解

The English alphabet has 26 letters, and every word you will ever read is built from these 26 friends. Learning to recognise them is the very first step in reading English.

Each letter comes in two forms:

  • Upper case (大写): A, B, C … — used at the start of a sentence and for names.
  • Lower case (小写): a, b, c … — used for most of the writing you will do.

A handy chant to remember the five vowels: "A, E, I, O, U — and sometimes Y." All the other 21 letters are consonants.

Real-life tip: English is everywhere — on your T-shirt, your cereal box, the traffic sign ("STOP"), and the app icons on a tablet. Whenever you spot a letter, say its name and sound out loud. That turns the whole world into your classroom!

✏️ 例题精讲

基础：辨认大小写

题目：Write the lower case of: G, M, R, T, W.

解答：g, m, r, t, w.

💡 Say the letter, then write both forms together: "G g, M m" — it helps your brain link the pair.

进阶：按顺序背字母

题目：Which letter comes after P? Which letter comes before M?

解答：After P comes Q. Before M comes L.

💡 Sing the alphabet song slowly and point to each letter on a chart — music makes memorising easy.

挑战：在生活中找字母

题目：Look around your room and find 5 things with English letters on them. Say the first letter of each item.

解答：Sample: the word "Milk" on a carton starts with M; "Rice" starts with R; "STOP" on a card starts with S.

💡 Turn it into a daily game: every time you see English outside, shout the first letter!

🧩 亲子互动

🔤 "字母身体操" Alphabet Body Game（约 6 分钟）

  1. 家长随机说一个字母，孩子用身体摆出它的大写形状（如 T 形站立、S 形扭动），家长猜是哪个字母。
  2. 交换角色：孩子说字母，家长来摆，看谁摆得最像、猜得最快。
  3. 最后一起唱《字母歌》，每唱到一个字母就在空中用手指"写"出它的小写。
  4. 挑战：家长说一个单词（如 "cat"），孩子依次摆出 c、a、t 三个字母。

💡 目的：把字母形状和名称通过全身动作内化，让孩子"用身体记住字母"，特别适合低年级动觉学习者。

📝 课后练习

第 1 题：英语字母表一共有多少个字母？

✅ 答案：26 个。

解析：从 A 到 Z 共 26 个字母。

第 2 题：写出字母表中紧邻在 F 前后的两个字母。

✅ 答案：E 和 G

解析：顺序是 E, F, G。

第 3 题：找出下列字母中的元音字母：B, E, K, O, Y。

✅ 答案：E 和 O（Y 有时作元音，但基本元音是 E 和 O）。

解析：基本元音是 A, E, I, O, U。

第 4 题：你身边有哪些物品上印有英文字母？举出 3 个。

✅ 答案：开放性题，如书本封面、文具盒、零食包装。

解析：鼓励孩子在生活中主动发现英语，建立"英语在身边"的意识。
