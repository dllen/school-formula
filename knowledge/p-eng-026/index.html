形容词比较级

英语

bigger/biggest等比较级与最高级变化。

系统教程

本课目标

  • Can form comparatives with "-er" for short adjectives (taller, bigger, faster)
  • Can form superlatives with "-est" for short adjectives (tallest, biggest, fastest)
  • Can use "more / the most" for longer adjectives (more beautiful)
  • Can compare two things using "… than …" and three or more using "the … in / of"

知识讲解

When you want to compare things — who is taller, which is bigger, what is the best — you need the comparative and superlative forms of adjectives. English changes the adjective to do this.

Short adjectives (1 syllable, or 2 ending in -y): add -er / -est

  • tall → taller → tallest
  • big → bigger → biggest (double the g!)
  • fast → faster → fastest
  • happy → happier → happiest (y → i)

Longer adjectives (2+ syllables, not ending in -y): use more / the most

  • beautiful → more beautiful → the most beautiful
  • interesting → more interesting → the most interesting

Irregular ones to memorise:

  • good → better → the best
  • bad → worse → the worst
  • many/much → more → the most

How to use them:

  • Comparative (2 things): "Tom is taller than Lily."
  • Superlative (3+ things): "Tom is the tallest in the class."

Real-life tip: compare things at home — "The elephant is bigger than the dog. The blue whale is the biggest animal."

例题精讲

基础：写出比较级和最高级

题目：Write the comparative and superlative of: tall, big, happy.

解答：tall → taller → tallest; big → bigger → biggest; happy → happier → happiest.

big 双写 g；happy 变 y 为 i；tall 直接加 -er/-est。

进阶：用 than 比较

题目：Compare a cheetah and a turtle: fast.

解答："The cheetah is faster than the turtle."

两者比较用 比较级 + than。

挑战：用最高级造句

题目：Say that the Yangtze River is the longest river in China.

解答："The Yangtze River is the longest river in China."

三者及以上比较用 the + 最高级 + in/of…。

亲子互动

📏 "比比看" Compare It!（约 8 分钟）

  1. 家长拿出三本书（大、中、小），孩子用英语排序："big / bigger / the biggest"，并造句 "The red book is bigger than the blue one."
  2. 进阶：比身高——孩子和家长站一起，说 "My dad is taller than me. He is the tallest in my family."
  3. 挑战：家长说一个形容词（如 beautiful），孩子说出比较级和最高级，再用最高级说一个真实事实（"the most beautiful city"）。
  4. 特别轮：专门练不规则变化 good→better→best, bad→worst。

💡 目的：把比较级/最高级和实物对比结合，让抽象的语法变成看得见的大小、高矮。

课后练习

第 1 题：写出下列形容词的比较级和最高级：hot, nice, good。

答案：hotter/hottest; nicer/nicest; better/best

解析：hot 双写 t；nice 直接加 r/st；good 是不规则变化。

第 2 题：用 than 比较：This book is interesting. That book is more interesting.

答案：That book is more interesting than this one.

解析：两者比较用 比较级 + than。

第 3 题：改错：He is the taller in the class.

答案：He is the tallest in the class.

解析：三者及以上用最高级 tallest，不是比较级 taller。

第 4 题：写出 bad 的最高级。

答案：the worst

解析：bad 是不规则变化：bad → worse → worst。
