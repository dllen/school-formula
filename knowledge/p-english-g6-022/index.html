was 与 were

英语

用 was 和 were 表示过去的状态，用 There was 表示过去有。

语法grade-6cnm:cne_p193ces-2022:2.GR.08

📚 系统教程

🎯 本课目标

  • 区分 was 和 were 的使用对象（单数 vs 复数）
  • 掌握 There was/were 表示“过去有”的句型
  • 能够在句子中正确填入 was 或 were
  • 能够将现在时 is/are 转换为过去时 was/were

📖 知识讲解

When we talk about the past (过去), we use was and were instead of am, is, are.

  1. was：用于单数主语（I, he, she, it, a cat, the book）。 *Example: I was a student. (我以前是个学生。)
  2. were：用于复数主语（we, you, they, two cats, the books）。 *Example: They were happy. (他们那时很快乐。)
  3. There was/were：表示“过去有”。
    • There was + 单数名词 (e.g., There was a tree here.)
    • There were + 复数名词 (e.g., There were many trees here.)

✏️ 例题精讲

主谓一致

题目：The weather ____ sunny yesterday. (填 was 或 were)

解答：was

💡 The weather 是单数，所以用 was。

复数主语

题目：We ____ in the playground two hours ago. (填 was 或 were)

解答：were

💡 We 是复数，所以用 were。

There be 结构

题目：There ____ no computers in my school ten years ago. (填 was 或 were)

解答：were

💡 computers 是复数，因此用 There were。

📝 课后练习

第 1 题：I ____ (be) very shy when I was seven. (用正确形式填空)

✅ 答案：was

解析：主语 I 对应过去时用 was。

第 2 题：The students ____ excited about the trip. (填 was 或 were)

✅ 答案：were

解析：The students 是复数主语。

第 3 题：There ____ a small shop next to my house last year. (填 was 或 were)

✅ 答案：was

解析：a small shop 是单数。

第 4 题：You ____ late for school this morning. (填 was 或 were)

✅ 答案：were

解析：无论 you 是单数还是复数，be 动词过去时一律用 were。
