日常问候

英语

Hello/Good morning等日常交际用语。

📚 系统教程

🎯 本课目标

  • Can greet people appropriately by time of day: Good morning / afternoon / evening
  • Can ask "How are you?" and reply with "I'm fine, thank you"
  • Can say goodbye with "Goodbye / See you later"
  • Can use greetings naturally when meeting English teachers or visitors

📖 知识讲解

Greetings are the handshake of language — they start every conversation on a friendly note. In English, the greeting you choose often depends on the time of day.

  • Morning (before about 12:00): "Good morning!"
  • Afternoon (12:00–about 18:00): "Good afternoon!"
  • Evening (after about 18:00): "Good evening!"
  • Any time, informal: "Hello!" or "Hi!"

After the hello, people often check in with each other:

  • "How are you?" → "I'm fine, thank you. And you?" (or "I'm great!", "I'm OK.")
  • Remember: this is usually a greeting, not a real question about your health — a short, positive answer is perfect.

When it is time to part:

  • "Goodbye!" / "Bye!"
  • "See you later." / "See you tomorrow."

Real-life tip: every time you see your English teacher at school, try saying "Good morning, teacher!" It is a small habit that builds big confidence.

✏️ 例题精讲

基础：按时间选问候

题目：It is 7:30 in the morning. You meet your mum. What do you say?

解答："Good morning, Mum!" (We use "Good morning" before noon.)

💡 Match the greeting to the clock: before 12 → morning; 12–6 → afternoon; after 6 → evening.

进阶：完成问候对话

题目：A: "Hi! How are you?" B: "______"

解答："I'm fine, thank you. And you?"

💡 Always thank the person ("thank you") and return the question ("And you?").

挑战：情景选择

题目：School is over. You are leaving your friend. Which is the best goodbye? (a) Good morning (b) See you tomorrow (c) How are you

解答：(b) "See you tomorrow." — a natural goodbye when you will meet again.

💡 Good morning = hello at morning; See you… = goodbye; How are you = check-in question.

🧩 亲子互动

👋 "问候接力赛" Greeting Relay（约 6 分钟）

  1. 家长和孩子站成一排，家长举起"时间卡"（太阳=早上、中午太阳=下午、月亮=晚上），孩子立刻说出对应的问候语。
  2. 家长说 "How are you?"，孩子回答并反问 "And you?"，家长再回答，完成一个完整来回。
  3. 加入"道别"环节：家长举起"放学"图片，孩子说出道别语（"See you tomorrow!"）。
  4. 挑战：全程不许说中文，连续完成 5 轮不卡顿。

💡 目的：把问候语变成"条件反射"，让孩子在真实场景中脱口而出。

📝 课后练习

第 1 题：下午 3 点见到老师，你应该说什么？

✅ 答案：Good afternoon, teacher!

解析：afternoon（下午）用 Good afternoon。

第 2 题：回答：— How are you? — ______

✅ 答案：I'm fine, thank you.

解析：这是 How are you 的标准友好回答。

第 3 题：放学时和同学道别，用英语怎么说？

✅ 答案：Goodbye! / See you tomorrow!

解析：道别用 Goodbye 或 See you…。

第 4 题：判断对错：晚上见到爸爸时说 "Good morning, Dad!"

✅ 答案：错，晚上应该说 Good evening。

解析：晚上用 evening，早上才用 morning。
