人称代词

英语

I/you/he/she/they等代词的使用。

📚 系统教程

🎯 本课目标

  • Can use the seven subject pronouns: I, you, he, she, it, we, they
  • Can match each pronoun to the right person or thing (I = myself, he = a boy, it = an animal/thing)
  • Can replace a noun with the correct pronoun in a sentence
  • Can tell the difference between "he" (a male person) and "she" (a female person)

📖 知识讲解

Pronouns are tiny words that do a big job — they replace names and nouns so we do not have to repeat them. Imagine saying "Lily is 8. Lily is a student. Lily likes reading." That sounds tiring! With pronouns: "Lily is 8. She is a student. She likes reading."

The seven subject pronouns:

| Pronoun | When to use | Example | |---------|-------------|---------| | I | talking about myself | I am a student. | | you | talking to one person or many | You are my friend. | | he | one male person (boy/man) | He is tall. | | she | one female person (girl/woman) | She is kind. | | it | one animal or thing | It is a cat. | | we | I + other people | We are happy. | | they | more than one person, animal or thing | They are teachers. |

Quick trick: he = boy, she = girl, it = thing/animal, they = more than one.

Real-life tip: when you see a picture of a boy, point and say "He is …"; of a girl, "She is …"; of a dog, "It is …".

✏️ 例题精讲

基础：选择合适的代词

题目：Replace the underlined word with a pronoun: "Tom is a student. _____ is 10."

解答："He." Tom is a boy → use "he". → He is 10.

💡 A boy or man → he; a girl or woman → she; a thing/animal → it.

进阶：代词与名词配对

题目：Choose the right pronoun: "My mum and I are at home. _____ are cooking."

解答："We." "My mum and I" includes myself → use "we".

💡 "I + 别人" = we; "he/she/it 单独" = he/she/it; "两个以上" = they.

挑战：用代词改写句子

题目：Rewrite using a pronoun: "The apples are red."

解答："They are red." (More than one apple → they.)

💡 Plural nouns (apples, books, cats) → they; singular things → it.

🧩 亲子互动

👆 "指一指、说一说" Point and Say（约 6 分钟）

  1. 家长指着自己说 "I"，指着孩子说 "you"，指着爸爸的照片说 "he"，指着妈妈的照片说 "she"，指着家里的猫/玩具说 "it"。
  2. 孩子反过来指并说出代词，家长判断对错。
  3. 进阶：家长说 "the boys / the girls / the books"，孩子说出 "they"。
  4. 挑战：用 5 个不同的代词各造一个句子，连成一段小介绍。

💡 目的：把抽象的代词变成"这个人/那个东西"的具体指向，建立直观理解。

📝 课后练习

第 1 题："My sister is kind." 用代词替换 My sister。

✅ 答案：She is kind.

解析：sister 是女性，用 she。

第 2 题："The dog is cute." 用代词替换 The dog。

✅ 答案：It is cute.

解析：dog 是动物，用 it。

第 3 题："My friends and I" 用哪个代词替换？

✅ 答案：We

解析：I + 别人 = we。

第 4 题：判断："he" 可以用来指一只猫吗？

✅ 答案：一般不用，动物用 it；但如果知道是公猫且当宠物家庭成员，口语中偶尔也用 he/she。

解析：普通情况下动物用 it，宠物有时用 he/she 表示性别或亲近。
